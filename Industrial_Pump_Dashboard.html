Key Findings Summary

Critical Discovery

Maintenance decisions are COMPLETELY INDEPENDENT of sensor readings. All sensors show negligible correlations (|r|<0.015) with maintenance.

Fleet Status

Homogeneous fleet operation with <2% variation across 5 pumps. All pumps maintain similar maintenance rates (48.6%-50.6%).

Lifecycle Pattern

No degradation observed with operational hours. Contradicts typical industrial wear patterns - expected bathtub curve not observed.

Model Performance

All ML algorithms (Linear, Tree-based, Boosted) perform at ~50% baseline. This proves the problem is data quality, not model selection.

Risk Scoring

Risk score has ZERO discriminative power. All risk categories show ~50% maintenance rate despite proper statistical distribution.

Data Nature

Likely synthetic data (most probable), externally scheduled maintenance, or failure-driven with unpredictable catastrophic events.
